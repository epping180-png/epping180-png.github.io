Childcare Archive

A personal tool for archiving childcare updates and photos from the account owner's childcare portal to their private Google Drive. It runs scheduled collection and produces rule-based highlights for parent actions, health and wellbeing, and learning activities.

個人托育紀錄備份工具，將帳戶擁有者托育平台上的更新與照片備份至其私人 Google 雲端硬碟。程式定期擷取資料，並依預設規則標示家長待辦事項、健康與身心狀況，以及學習活動。

Privacy information · Use information

隱私資訊 · 使用資訊

Privacy information / 隱私資訊

The tool requests only Google's drive.file permission. It creates and manages archive files in Google Drive, and can access files the owner explicitly opens or shares with the tool. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Photos, or the owner's entire Drive.

本工具僅要求 Google 的 drive.file 權限，用於建立及管理雲端硬碟備份檔案，以及存取擁有者明確以本工具開啟或授權的檔案。它不要求 Gmail、Google 相簿或整個雲端硬碟的存取權。

Google authorization credentials and childcare login credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets. GitHub's hosted runners process the childcare content during collection and upload it to the owner's Google Drive. Archive content is not intended to be published in repositories or workflow logs. Setup inspection files may also be saved privately in the archive folder while the collector is being validated.

Google 授權憑證與托育平台登入憑證儲存為 GitHub Actions 程式庫的加密密鑰。GitHub 的雲端執行環境在擷取過程中處理托育內容，並將內容上傳至擁有者的 Google 雲端硬碟。備份內容不會刻意公開於程式庫或工作流程紀錄；驗證備份程式期間，設定檢查檔案也可能私下儲存於備份資料夾。

The owner controls retention and sharing in Drive and may revoke Google access through their Google Account connections page, remove the GitHub secrets, or disable the workflow. Revoking authorization does not delete existing archive files. No advertising, sale of content, or AI model training is part of this tool's operation.

擁有者可在雲端硬碟控制資料保留與分享設定，也可透過 Google 帳戶的連線頁面撤銷授權、移除 GitHub 密鑰，或停用工作流程。撤銷授權不會刪除既有備份檔案。本工具不以內容投放廣告、販售內容或訓練 AI 模型。

Use information / 使用資訊

This is a personal utility for its account owner, not a public childcare service. The owner should archive only content they are authorized to access. Highlights are keyword-based excerpts and should be checked against the original posts. They do not establish a child's individual participation, medical condition, or developmental assessment. Original records remain the source of truth.

這是供帳戶擁有者使用的個人工具，不是公開托育服務。擁有者應僅備份其有權存取的內容。重點標示依關鍵字擷取原文，應對照原始貼文確認；它不代表孩子個別參與活動的證明、健康狀況判定或發展評估。原始紀錄仍是查核依據。

Collection depends on the childcare website, Google authorization, and GitHub scheduling. Changes or outages can require maintenance or renewed authorization. Check the archive and workflow status to confirm successful runs.

資料擷取依賴托育網站、Google 授權和 GitHub 排程。服務變更或中斷可能需要維護或重新授權，應查看備份與工作流程狀態，確認執行成功。
